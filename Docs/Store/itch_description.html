THREATS ARE HIDING AMONG US. YOUR CITY NEEDS YOU.

Welcome to the City Surveillance Network Training Division. You've been assigned a drone, a phone and a list of suspicious individuals. Identify the threat, wait for the right moment, and press the button.

The city has prepared some helpful tools: a falling piano, a traffic light, an open sewer, a hotel elevator, two drones, a remote-controlled car and a kennel full of very motivated dogs.

Score 1000 points to graduate. Fail too often and the system may decide that you are the threat.

FEATURES

  • 8 training missions — one button (and two arrows for the RC car)
  • Read the target card, find the right person, time your move
  • A drone of your own that gets certified... or destroyed
  • Sponsored messages from our partners (skippable, eventually)
  • An ending the Agency would rather you didn't see

CONTROLS

  • Space / Click / Tap — act
  • A / D or ← / → — steer the RC car

SUPPORT THE AGENCY
